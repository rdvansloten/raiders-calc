The Splatchet is a Power-type gadget in Splatoon Raiders.

Appearance

The Splatchet resembles an axe or hatchet, as the name suggests. The "blade" is formed from ink emitted from a fan-shaped nozzle, which is attached to a short hose and a curved handle.

Description

The Splatchet is an axe-like weapon that, when used, has the user charge forward and create a big slash, which is able to pierce through Salmonid armor. The speed and distance of the attack are similar to the Decavitator and Grizzco Splatana weapons in Splatoon 3, especially the latter.

Etymology

The name is a portmanteau of "splat" and "hatchet", which is a one-handed axe.
