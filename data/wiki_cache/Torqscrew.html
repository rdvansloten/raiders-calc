Description

The Torqscrew is made up of a large propeller with a hose connected to a gas canister attached, and anchored by a suction cup. Its propeller deals damage and creates a large vortex that also deals damage. It can be attached to walls.

Gadget Parts

The Torqscrew can be equipped with a variety of Gadget Parts to improve its capabilities, such as causing players in its vortex to float upwards.

Etymology

The name is a portmanteau of "torque", a twisting force, and "corkscrew".
