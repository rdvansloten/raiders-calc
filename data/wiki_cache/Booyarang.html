Description

The Booyarang is a boomerang-like weapon that, when used, will travel in the direction thrown and spin in the air whilst attacking Salmonids. It will return to the user after a few seconds, after which it will need to wait before being used again. Its appearance is a fan that is attached to a Splat Dualie and hoses taped to the wings. The exact stats are currently unknown.

Etymology

The name is a portmanteau of "booyah" and "boomerang".
