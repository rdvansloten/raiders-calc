Description

The Bombloons are a Tactic-type gadget that float in the air. Multiple can be placed at once. When at least two are placed, stretchy ink connects each Bombloon. Once the player shoots one Bombloon, it can trigger the rest of the Bombloon(s) to explode. They resemble some sort of buoy, beach ball, or balloon with ropes keeping it together. They float with a very small metal propeller that has a green light bulb on top.
